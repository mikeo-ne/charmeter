# Phase 3 — Regional Promotion & Distribution Playbook

Research converts to traction only through three regional levers: **DJs**, **cross-border
features**, and **community touchpoints**.

---

## 1. DJ Mix & Club Saturation

### The seeding workflow

**T-21 days — Prepare the pack.** One folder, one link, no logins required:
`Artist_Track_Clean.wav` (24-bit), `..._Radio.mp3` (320), `..._Instrumental.mp3`,
`..._DJ_Intro.mp3` (8-bar spoken drop, personalisable), cover art 3000×3000, a 60-word
bio, and a one-line "why this works in your set" note.

**T-14 — Tier the DJ list.** From `data/radar.yml`:
- *Tier 1*: DJs who have played your previous material. Personal voice note, not text.
- *Tier 2*: Genre-aligned DJs at venues where your peers get rotation. Warm intro preferred.
- *Tier 3*: Mobile/mixtape DJs and digital mix channels. Bulk pack, still personalised greeting.

**T-10 — Seed Tier 1** with an exclusive window ("yours for 5 days before anyone else").

**T-7 — Seed Tier 2 and 3.** Include the DJ drop; it converts because it makes the DJ sound
like the source.

**T-0 — Release.** Ask Tier 1 for a clip of the crowd reaction, not for a repost.

**T+7 — Harvest.** Log every confirmed spin in the tracker. Crowd-reaction clips become your
next two weeks of short-form content and your strongest proof for radio and playlist pitches.

**T+21 — Second wave.** Send the instrumental to mixtape DJs for blends and refixes; a refix
restarts the track's life cycle at near-zero cost.

### Coverage targets per release

| Market | Club DJs | Mobile/mixtape DJs | Digital mix channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kampala | 12 | 8 | 4 |
| Nairobi | 8 | 5 | 3 |
| Dar es Salaam | 6 | 4 | 3 |

Track with [`../templates/dj-seeding-tracker.md`](../templates/dj-seeding-tracker.md).
Rule: **no DJ is contacted twice without an outcome logged** from the first contact.

## 2. Cross-Border Collaboration

A feature is a distribution deal disguised as a song. Select for **audience complementarity**,
not for clout.

### Selection scoring (score each candidate 1–5, take the highest total)

| Criterion | Weight | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Audience overlap | ×1 (lower is better — invert) | <25% shared listeners |
| Tier parity | ×2 | Within one tier; never more than 5× your listener count |
| Subgenre bridge | ×2 | Their tag is adjacent, letting the record live in two playlist worlds |
| Market access | ×3 | They have real editorial/radio relationships in the target country |
| Work rate | ×2 | Ships on time, promotes their own features, has a live show |

### Typical bridges

- **Luganda pop → Kenya:** pair with a gengetone or Kenyan-afropop act; Sheng hook over a
  Luganda verse reads as novelty in Nairobi and as ambition in Kampala.
- **Kampala → Dar es Salaam:** bongo flava vocalist on the bridge; Tanzanian radio strongly
  favours records with a local voice on them.
- **EA Amapiano → regional:** producer-led collabs travel further than vocal features — a
  named Tanzanian or South-African-adjacent producer credit unlocks playlist consideration.

### Deal hygiene (agree before the session)

Splits in writing; who distributes and who owns the master; two named promo obligations each
(e.g. one joint IG live, one radio round in the partner's city); asset delivery dates;
territory-specific release timing.

## 3. Community-First Engagement

### WhatsApp broadcast lists

The highest-conversion channel in the region and the least used deliberately.

- Segment: `Core-Fans`, `DJs`, `Media`, `Street-Team`, `Family-Friends`. Never one blast to all.
- Cadence: max 2 broadcasts/week to fans; 1 per release cycle to media.
- Format: 1 voice note (≤30s, personal) + 1 asset (link or status-ready 9:16 clip) + 1 explicit ask.
- The ask must be single and tiny: "post this on your status today."
- Measure: replies and status reposts, not delivery counts.

### Live listening spaces

- Pre-release: 20–40 person listening session, phones allowed, one track played three times.
- Capture: audience reaction audio; it is the best pre-release content you will get for free.
- Post-release: weekly IG/X live, fixed day and time — consistency beats production value.

### Youth-focused underground events

- Campus halls, barbershops, car washes, sports viewing nights, skate/BMX and art meet-ups.
- Offer: perform two songs free in exchange for the guest list and a filming slot.
- Convert: QR or short link on screen → WhatsApp list, not a follow button.

### The loop

```
Event / live space  ->  WhatsApp list growth  ->  Status reposts  ->  TikTok sound re-use
        ^                                                                    |
        +------------ DJ hears demand, adds to set, invites to gig <---------+
```

Every tactic in this playbook exists to keep that loop turning. Anything that doesn't feed it
is a vanity activity.
