Built to stop you measuring yourself against the wrong people

Tier-matched, always

An artist at 7,000 listeners is never benchmarked against one at 145,000. Chartmeter splits Upcoming (0–20k) from Mid-level (20k–500k) and refuses to mix them.

Same subgenre only

Luganda pop is compared to Luganda pop. Amapiano acts are marked tactics only — useful to learn from, excluded from your numbers.

Median, not maximum

You're measured against the middle of your peer set, not its best act. One outlier should never set your targets.

Every gap has an action

"DJ spins 33% below peers" comes with the seeding workflow to fix it — not just a red number on a chart.

Honest about the data

Spotify doesn't expose monthly listeners to anyone without 250k+ users. We tell you that, and never fake it, rather than scraping numbers that break.

The regional reality

DJ rotation, WhatsApp broadcast lists and radio adds are tracked as first-class metrics, because in this market they move more than playlists.
